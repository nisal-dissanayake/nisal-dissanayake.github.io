Questions

Does it work on a phone?

You can log transactions in the Google Sheets or Excel mobile apps. The dashboard is easiest to read on a computer or tablet.

Can I add my own categories?

Yes. Fill the empty rows on the Setup tab and they appear everywhere automatically.

Does it only work for 2027?

No. Change the year in one cell on the Setup tab and every date and report follows.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx file to Google Drive and choose "Open with Google Sheets". Formulas, dropdowns and charts work. You need a free Google account.

Which currency does it use?

Any. Amounts are plain numbers, so use whatever currency you earn and spend in.
