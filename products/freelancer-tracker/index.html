Questions

What tax rate should I use?

That's up to you. It should combine your income tax and any self-employment or social-security tax, which vary by country and income. The sheet does the arithmetic; check your rate with a tax professional or your local tax authority.

Does it file my taxes?

No. It tracks income and expenses and estimates how much to set aside. It doesn't prepare or file a return.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx file to Google Drive and choose "Open with Google Sheets". Formulas, dropdowns and charts work. You need a free Google account.

Which currency does it use?

Any. Amounts are plain numbers, so use whatever currency you earn and spend in.

How do I get the file?

Right after checkout you get a download link by email. Checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's a planning and tracking tool based on the numbers you enter.
