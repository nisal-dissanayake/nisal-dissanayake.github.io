Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx file to Google Drive and choose "Open with Google Sheets". Formulas, dropdowns and charts work. You need a free Google account.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes, in Excel for Mac, Excel on the web, or Google Sheets in any browser. No macros or add-ons are used.

Which currency does it use?

Any. Amounts are plain numbers, so use whatever currency you earn and spend in.

How do I get the file?

Right after checkout you get a download link by email. Checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy.

Is my financial data private?

Yes. The spreadsheet is a file you keep. Nothing connects to your bank and nothing you type is sent to us.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are planning and tracking tools based on the numbers you enter. For advice on your situation, talk to a qualified professional.
